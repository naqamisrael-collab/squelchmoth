Squelchmoth is installed

Open it from your Applications folder (or Launchpad).

First launch: pick your audio interface in Setup > Playback Engine. Your VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins are found automatically.

Have a licence key? Help > Enter Licence Key...

Your work is saved automatically (Auto-Save every 2 minutes, plus Session File Backups). To remove Squelchmoth later, open Uninstall Squelchmoth in Applications.
