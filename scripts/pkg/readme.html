Before you install

Needs: macOS 11 Big Sur or later, an Intel or Apple Silicon Mac, 4 GB RAM (8 GB+ recommended), 1 GB free disk space.

What gets installed: Squelchmoth.app and Uninstall Squelchmoth.app in your Applications folder. Nothing else is installed system-wide - no drivers, no background services, no login items.

Your files: sessions are saved in Documents/Squelchmoth Sessions, with automatic Session File Backups next to them. Insert chains and presets live in Documents/Squelchmoth.

Free trial: 30 days with every feature. After that, saving and bouncing need a licence key; opening and playing sessions keep working.

Plug-ins: your VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins are found automatically on first launch.

Uninstalling: open Uninstall Squelchmoth in Applications (or Help > Uninstall Squelchmoth... in the app). Your sessions are kept unless you choose to remove them.
