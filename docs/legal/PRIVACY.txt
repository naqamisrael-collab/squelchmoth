SQUELCHMOTH PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.9 - 2026. Squelchmoth is created and owned by Jamar Stewart.

Short version: Squelchmoth does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your music stays on your computer.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Sessions, recordings, bounces, stems, presets, insert chains, mix snapshots and backups.
- Preferences, the plug-in list, crash logs and the activity log (Help > Copy diagnostics copies them only when you ask).
- Your licence key. Keys are checked on your computer with a digital signature - nothing is sent anywhere to activate.

NO ANALYTICS, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
Squelchmoth contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and sends no usage statistics.

WHEN SQUELCHMOTH USES THE INTERNET
Only when you choose to:
1. Connect Claude (Claude menu > Connect Claude). Squelchmoth starts a private, encrypted link (a Cloudflare tunnel, or
   ngrok if you entered your own ngrok token) so that Claude can control the app. The link address contains a long
   random secret and stops working when you disconnect or quit. While connected, Claude can read what it asks for
   through Squelchmoth's tools - track names, clip positions, plug-in settings, meter readings and screenshots of the
   app - in order to do what you ask. Audio files are not uploaded unless you ask Claude to work with one.
   What you say to Claude is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy.
2. If the link helper is missing, Squelchmoth downloads it once from its official publisher (Cloudflare on GitHub, or
   ngrok) over HTTPS.
3. Help > Visit the Store, the Buy buttons in Help > Licences and Upgrades and similar items open web pages in your browser.
Third-party plug-ins you load may have their own internet features and privacy policies.

BUYING AND THE WEBSITE
The squelchmoth.com website has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. When you download the Demo or buy a product,
the store provider (Payhip) handles checkout: it receives your email address and payment details, emails your
download link and licence key, and handles tax, under Payhip's own privacy policy. The Licensor receives your name,
email address and what you bought, uses them to support your purchase and, only if you agree at checkout, to send
product news. You can unsubscribe from any email.

CHILDREN
Squelchmoth is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

YOUR CHOICES
Disconnect Claude at any time from the Claude menu. Uninstalling (Help > Uninstall Squelchmoth) removes the app, its
preferences, caches and logs; your sessions are kept unless you choose to remove them too.

CHANGES
If this policy changes, the new version ships with the next update and is shown in Help > Privacy Policy.
