SQUELCHMOTH - quick start
=========================
Created and owned by Jamar Stewart.

INSTALL ON WINDOWS (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)
1. Unzip Squelchmoth-Windows.zip and double-click "Squelchmoth-Setup".
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway". (Windows asks this
   because the installer is new and not yet widely downloaded.)
3. Click Yes to allow the installer, then Next > I Agree > Install > Finish. Squelchmoth opens, and it is in
   the Start menu and on the desktop.
4. If Windows Firewall asks about Squelchmoth or cloudflared, click Allow (only needed for the Claude link).
5. Audio: Setup > Playback Engine. Pick your interface under "Windows Audio" (WASAPI). Your VST3 plug-ins in
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 are found automatically.
6. Uninstall any time from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Squelchmoth. Your sessions stay.
7. Shortcuts: everywhere these guides say Cmd, use Ctrl on Windows; Opt is Alt.

INSTALL ON MAC
0. Intel Mac: use Squelchmoth-Mac-Intel.zip. M1/M2/M3/M4 Mac: Squelchmoth-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip.
   Works on macOS 11 Big Sur and newer.
1. Double-click the zip. Inside: Squelchmoth.app, "Install Squelchmoth", "Uninstall Squelchmoth" and the guides.
2. RIGHT-click (or Control-click) "Install Squelchmoth" and choose Open, then click Open again.
   (macOS asks this once because the app is not from the App Store. If there is no Open button:
   click OK, open System Preferences > Security & Privacy > General and click "Open Anyway".)
   It copies Squelchmoth into Applications and offers to open it. Or simply drag Squelchmoth.app into
   Applications yourself and right-click > Open it the first time.
3. An older version is replaced; your sessions, settings and licence key are kept.
4. Open Squelchmoth from Applications or Launchpad.
5. If macOS asks for microphone access, click OK (needed to record).
Squelchmoth works completely offline: recording, editing, mixing, plugins, kits, mastering, the
Demo and your licence keys never need the internet. Only Squelchmoth Chat and the Claude connection need a connection, and
it simply waits (status bar: "Offline") until you are online again.

UNINSTALL
- Open "Uninstall Squelchmoth" in Applications (or Help > Uninstall Squelchmoth...). It asks for your Mac
  password once and removes the app, its settings, caches and logs. Your sessions are kept unless you choose
  "Remove Everything" (then they go to the Trash, so you can still get them back).

LICENCE
- Without a key Squelchmoth is the free Demo, with no time limit: 16 tracks, the basic instruments and
  effects, kits 1-5, save and bounce your mix.
- To unlock what you bought: Help > Licences and Upgrades... > paste the key from your purchase email (starts
  with SQM2-; spaces and line breaks are ignored) > Activate. Enter one key per product; they stack.
- Sessions that use plug-ins you don't own yet open with "Locked" panels that keep every setting. After
  unlocking, click "Reopen session to unlock".
- Buy at https://squelchmoth.com: Squelchmoth $189.99 (list $299.99), Squelchmoth Complete $349.99, or the
  add-on packs (Vocal Suite, Mastering Suite, Instrument Collection, Beat Lab Expansion, Creative FX).

FIRST LAUNCH
- The Quick Start window offers New Session / Template / Open / Connect Claude / a 5-tip tour.
- Your VST3 and Audio Unit plugins are found automatically on every launch (status bar shows
  progress; Setup > Plugins Window for details and extra folders).
- Pick your audio interface: Setup > Playback Engine.
- Change the look (Edit/Mix window backgrounds, track colours, text size):
  Setup > Preferences > Display.  Window > Color Palette recolours selected tracks and clips.

SQUELCHMOTH CHAT (talk to Claude inside the app)
1. Click CHAT (top right) or press Ctrl+Shift+C (Cmd+Shift+C on a Mac). The chat opens beside your session.
2. Paste your Anthropic API key (console.anthropic.com > API keys) and press Verify. It is stored only on
   this computer. Anthropic bills API use to that account; a claude.ai Pro or Max plan does not cover it.
3. Type what you want and press Enter ("put a compressor on the vocal and level it to -14 LUFS"). Claude does
   it in the session and lists every tool it used; click a row to see the details. STOP halts it.
   Shift+Enter starts a new line. Earlier chats are under the hourglass button; + starts a new one.

THE CONSOLE (Mix window)
- Each strip has a width selector: Compact, Standard or Expanded. Channel Layout sets them all at once and
  saves named layouts with the session. View: Dynamics (the console), Full (adds inserts, sends, I/O, pan,
  R / I / polarity / mono) or Overview (every strip Compact).
- COMP (cyan) and LIMIT (pink) choose the channel's compressor and limiter; their meters show real gain
  reduction (n/a when a plug-in does not report it). Expanded strips choose the meter: Peak, RMS or LUFS.
- Select several strips (Shift-click) and drag one fader: they all move by the same amount. Hold Alt
  (Opt on a Mac) to move just one. Drag a strip's name sideways to reorder tracks.

CONNECT CLAUDE (use Squelchmoth from claude.ai instead)
1. Click CLAUDE (top right). Five status lights show each step.
2. Click CONNECT CLAUDE. Wait until the fourth light is green ("verified: the internet can
   reach Squelchmoth").
3. Click COPY LINK.
4. Click OPEN CLAUDE CONNECTORS (claude.ai > Settings > Connectors). Click "Add custom connector".
   Name: Squelchmoth. Paste the link. Leave sign-in / OAuth empty. Click Add.
5. In a Claude chat click the + (tools) button > Connectors and turn Squelchmoth on. Ask away.

IF IT DOES NOT CONNECT
- Claude menu > Run connection self-test tells you which stage failed.
- If the public link never verifies, your network blocks Cloudflare (port 7844): change the
  "Link provider" menu in the CLAUDE panel to ngrok (free account, works everywhere, fixed
  address) or Pinggy (no account). Then press CONNECT CLAUDE again and copy the new link.
- Claude menu > Copy diagnostics for support, then paste into your Claude chat for exact help.
- Custom connectors need a Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan.

The Cloudflare / Pinggy link changes every time you connect. After restarting Squelchmoth, edit
the Squelchmoth connector on claude.ai and paste the new link (ngrok with a static domain never changes).

MAKE A BEAT IN 60 SECONDS (the FL Studio way, 1.8)
1. File > Make a Beat (or the Quick Start window's "Make a Beat" button). You get a new session, a kit in the
   Channel Rack, the Beat page (EDIT - MIX - BEAT - CLAUDE on the transport; Cmd+Shift+B) and the rack open
   on top as its own window (Cmd+6 brings it back any time).
2. Click steps in the rack: Kick on 1, 5, 9, 13; Snare on 5 and 13; hats on every other step. Or press
   BEAT STARTER, pick a genre, and it writes the groove for you. Press Space.
3. Press + at the bottom of the rack, pick an instrument (Mothsynth 2, Venom, Mini Moth, Dial 10, Blossom 2,
   Moth 808...). Click its channel button to open it and pick a preset.
4. Press PIANO ROLL on the new channel, then Progression Tool: pick a key, a genre, click a progression,
   Preview, Insert. (Or Chord stamp: pick a chord type, click a key.)
5. In the Playlist (centre of the Beat page), click where you want the pattern - every channel gets a linked
   Pattern Clip. Press + in the rack's pattern selector for Pattern 2, change it, place it after the first.
   PAT loops the pattern you're editing; SONG plays the arrangement.
6. File > Bounce Mix. Done. The same session is right there in the EDIT and MIX pages - every rack channel is a
   track - and View > Layout > Edit + Mix shows both at once.
Say it to Claude instead: "make me a 140 BPM drill beat with an 808 and a dark piano progression in F minor"
- it uses rack, pattern, chords, instrument and bounce.

IN 1.7 ("Session" - playback, the Edit window, autosave, and sale-ready)
- Playback and gain staging fixed: a stereo file on a stereo track at 0 dB plays bit-exact (before, the Master
  and stereo faders were 3-6 dB quiet). Mono tracks are really mono; an empty mono track that gets a stereo file
  turns stereo by itself. Old sessions are corrected when you open them.
- One clip sounds at a time on a track, like Pro Tools: a clip dropped, moved or recorded over another covers it.
- Edit modes rebuilt to Pro Tools behaviour: Shuffle re-orders clips like tiles and ripples trims and deletes;
  Shuffle Lock (Cmd-click Shuffle); Slip is free (Cmd-drag snaps); Spot opens the Spot dialog with Original and User
  Time Stamps; Grid is Absolute or Relative (REL) and follows tempo and meter changes; Shift-click Grid combines it
  with Shuffle or Slip (+GRID).
- Rulers: Bars|Beats, Min:Secs, Timecode (23.976-30 fps, drop-frame), Samples, Tempo, Meter and Markers - click the
  ruler names to show or hide them. Drag a tempo up or down, double-click to add tempos and meters.
  Main and sub counters in any time format.
- Transport: Stop goes back to where you started (INS FOLLOW turns that off); playing a selection stops at its
  end; the playhead moves at screen refresh rate with Page, Continuous or No scrolling.
- Tracks: Micro to Extreme heights and Fit to Window (Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down), a polarity (phase) button on every
  track and mixer strip, Peak / Rectified / Power waveforms, and the Smart Tool crossfade zone (bottom corner where

- Insert chains: save every plug-in on a track as one chain and load it on any track (click an insert slot).

  default), timecode rate and start, pan depth. New Session asks for the sample rate and bit depth
