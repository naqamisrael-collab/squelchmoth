# Squelchmoth built-in plugin suite

Squelchmoth ships **the Signature bundle (123 instruments and processors - 27 of them instruments)**, the complete
AirWindows collection (186 effects), 27 drum sound kits and **Cut Lab**, the audio editor. Everything is included,
loads with no extra licence, and can be inserted by name from the Mix window or by Claude (`insert_plugin`).
Names below are exactly what you type.

## Cut Lab (the audio editor)

Window → Cut Lab, a clip's menu → **Open in Cut Lab**, or Claude's `cut_lab` tool. Open any WAV / AIFF / FLAC or record
from your inputs, then select and edit: cut, copy, paste, trim, silence, fade in / out, normalise, gain, reverse,
remove DC; process: spectral denoise (learns the noise from the quietest frames), convolution with any impulse response,
granular blur, time stretch and pitch shift (independently); set loop points and markers (saved in the WAV), undo
everything, then **Send to Timeline** or drag the file out. Claude runs the same operations on files without opening the
window (`cut_lab edit` with a list of ops) and reads length, peak, RMS, loop and markers (`cut_lab info`).

## Factory content for the samplers and slicers

Everything is generated by Squelchmoth itself on first use (into Documents/Squelchmoth/Factory), from its own drum engine:

- **Chrysalis 2 / Chrysalis SF**: "Kits | <kit>" puts the twelve kit sounds on their keys; "808 | <kit> 808" plays the kit's
  808 across the keyboard, pitch-detected and tuned so every note is in tune. Chrysalis SF gets the same sounds as real
  SoundFont 2 banks (one per kit) that any SF2 player can open.
- **Mandible / Mandible Lite**: "<genre> | <kit> Loop" - two-bar loops of the Beat Lab genre grooves (Trap, Drill, Boom Bap,
  Lo-Fi, R&B, Afrobeats, Amapiano, Reggaeton, Jersey Club, House, Techno, Phonk, Pop, Drum & Bass, Dancehall, Rock,
  Funk / Neo-Soul), three kits each, with the genre's swing, ghosts and rolls. Mandible's deck B gets the same groove's
  second variation on the next kit.

## AudioSuite (rendered effects)

Clip menu → AudioSuite, or the AudioSuite menu: Normalise, Reverse, Invert, Make Mono, Pitch Shift, or render
any effect plugin onto a clip. Renders are non-destructive (a per-clip chain you can remove).

**Molt (Time & Pitch)** - AudioSuite → Molt, or Claude's `molt` tool. The offline, highest-quality time and pitch
renderer: change length by percent (to any decimal, e.g. 100.1 % for a 0.1 % pull-down), ratio,
seconds or tempo; shift pitch in semitones and cents with or without formant correction; Polyphonic, Monophonic
(formant-exact for vocals and bass) or Fast; multi-input rendering keeps several tracks phase-coherent. 15 presets,
audition before rendering, and the result replaces the clip as a new file - fully undoable.
